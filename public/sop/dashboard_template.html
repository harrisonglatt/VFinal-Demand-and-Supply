Calculations

Total Sales $ · sum across selected SKUs and weeks
Online Sales $ · sum over (Sales $ × Online Orig Pen) per SKU per week
Digital Penetration % · Online Sales $ ÷ Total Sales $
$PSPW (category) · sum of Sales ÷ sum of Stores Tracked across SKUs in category
Promo % (category) · sales-weighted average of per-SKU Promo % of Sales
OOS % (category) · sales-weighted average of per-SKU OOS %
Total ROAS · Total Sales ÷ Roundel Spend (correlation, not causal lift)
Online ROAS · Online Sales ÷ Roundel Spend
Cost / digital $ · Roundel Spend ÷ Online Sales
Promo Lift · (Total Sales − Base 4-wk Sales) ÷ Base 4-wk Sales
SKU → Category mapping · default mapping derived from item description; users override on the SKU Mappings page (saved locally)

Roundel Intelligence — baseline methodologies

Trailing 4w baseline · avg sales of the 4 weeks immediately before each supported week. Most reactive; sensitive to recent shocks (promo, OOS, weather).
Trailing 13w baseline · avg sales of the 13 weeks before each supported week. Smoothest; can over-credit Roundel during periods of overall growth.
Non-promo, non-supported baseline · avg of recent weeks where the cat had no Roundel spend AND no promo (≥ 20% promo % threshold). Cleanest causal signal — but requires enough qualifying weeks (falls back to trailing 13w if < 3).
Comparable cat baseline · uses the current week's avg sales of unsupported cats, scaled by the historical (last 26w) ratio of this cat to others on unsupported weeks. Controls for seasonality.
Caveat · all four are quasi-experimental. Holdout testing is the only causal proof. These baselines are decision-grade evidence, not causal proof.

Update / refresh process

  1. Click Upload data in the topbar.
  2. Drop the latest Target Sales & Inventory .xlsx + Roundel Spend .csv.
  3. Click Apply & rebuild — the dashboard re-extracts, re-computes, and re-renders. The Last week chip in the topbar updates automatically to the latest week in the upload.
  4. Uploaded data persists in browser localStorage. Use Reset to bundled data to discard.
  5. For programmatic refresh (replace upload step), see the Connector roadmap below.

Connector roadmap (auto-load)

Target Partners Online (POL) · weekly POS, Item Trends, Inventory exports — pull via Vendorful / POL API. No public endpoint; requires Target IT-coordinated SFTP feed or POL extract automation.
Circana / IRI · POS data feed. Direct API access available with subscription tier; recommend a nightly cron writing to S3 + a build step that re-extracts.
Target Roundel · campaign spend tracker. No public API; the in-platform export remains CSV. Roundel reps can grant Excel-on-OneDrive access (auto-syncs).
Run cadence · Target Circana refreshes weekly (Tuesday). Recommend a Wednesday-AM scheduled build that pulls fresh data, runs extract.js → compute.js → build.js, and redeploys.
